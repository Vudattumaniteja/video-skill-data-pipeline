## Focus

Prioritize moments where pacing, text, sound, and visual layout work together to keep attention.

Look for:

- 3-second visual resets
- audio hits synchronized with cuts or layout changes
- fast text overlays
- dense but readable screen states
- transitions between explanation modes
- screenshots, UI captures, charts, or product visuals used to clarify ideas

## Ignore Unless Exceptional

- slow premium documentary composition
- asset sourcing that is not tied to clarity or retention

## Evidence Reference Bias

Create references for sound-synced visual changes, data/text density, layout transitions, and retention moments.
