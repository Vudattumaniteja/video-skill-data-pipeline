Learn asset selection and asset sourcing for premium retro/documentary-style videos.

## Focus

Prioritize moments where the video uses archival material, premium visual texture, strong foreground/background selection, clean cutouts, or documentary composition.

Look for:

- historical imagery that feels specific rather than generic
- images chosen for emotion, era, texture, or symbolic meaning
- asset layering and depth
- before/after or bad-to-good visual replacement logic, if present
- restrained text and premium pacing

## Ignore Unless Exceptional

- dense text systems
- high-speed retention tricks
- generic sound-sync observations

## Evidence Reference Bias

Create most references for asset choice and asset sourcing. Layout references are optional when the composition teaches a reusable documentary layout rule.
