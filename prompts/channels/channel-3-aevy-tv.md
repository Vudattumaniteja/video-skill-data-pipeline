# Channel Module: Channel 3 Aevy TV

## Extraction Mission

Learn essay-style pacing, broad good-video principles, layout support, sound rhythm, and text/data clarity.

## Category Priorities

- What Makes a Good Video: 8.5
- Data & Text Density: 8.5
- How to Select Assets: 8.0
- Editing Framework / Layout: 8.0
- Sound Design Sync: 8.0
- Sourcing Great Assets: 7.5

## Focus

Prioritize how the video supports a narrative or argument while staying visually engaging.

Look for:

- visuals that support a thesis or explanation
- pacing changes between setup, context, and payoff
- cafe/essay style framing choices
- controlled text density
- layout changes that match argument structure
- sound rhythm that supports pacing rather than calling attention to itself

## Ignore Unless Exceptional

- isolated asset sourcing details with no impact on the idea
- purely decorative motion

## Evidence Reference Bias

Spread references across good-video principles, text density, asset selection, layout, and sound rhythm.
