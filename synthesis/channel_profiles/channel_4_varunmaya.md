## Repeated Patterns

### asset_selection
- **Embedded Social Proof (Tweets)**: Placing tweets from prominent figures (e.g. Sam Altman, Sachin Tendulkar, DiscussingFilm) directly onto the split layout as concrete proof surfaces.
- **Authority Footage & Product Details**: Incorporating video and close-ups from keynotes (Jensen Huang), developer updates (Tim Sweeney), or product close-ups (Apple-style design elements, Steam library).
- **Establishing Shot Resets**: Using brief wide-angle office/room shots (Microsoft office space showing host, guest, and branding) to break close-up monotony and establish physical workspace context.

### asset_sourcing
- **Physical Hardware Showcases**: Supporting technical claims with raw physical hardware/component close-ups (e.g., exposed onboard charger components and charging setup).

### good_video_principles
- **Recognizable Hook Anchors**: Launching videos with a close-up of a prominent guest/star (Satya Nadella), a design model reveal (Ferrari EV), or industry benchmarks (MacBook dominance) to capture immediate interest.
- **Narrative Reframing and Payoffs**: Concluding technical deep-dives with a thematic reframe (moving from motorcycle specs to a national tech-sovereignty narrative) or visual bookends (returning to the hook's Tweet overlay).
- **Presenter Reset Cuts**: Interrupting B-roll with a centered talking-head shot to transition the audience from detail review to counterarguments/caveats.

### editing_layout
- **Vertically Stacked Split Screens**: Stacking host and guest feeds (Satya Nadella/Varun, Sam Altman/Varun) to fit landscape webcam footage into a vertical 9:16 layout while maintaining simultaneous reaction visibility.
- **Rounded Floating Overlay Cards**: Framing tweets, articles, and external videos (Tim Sweeney, Twitter cards) in rounded-corner cards over the talking head, keeping presenter contact in the bottom half.
- **Stacked Comparison Layouts**: Side-by-side or stacked visual cards to display product benchmarks (Ferrari EV vs legacy, Nvidia Ray Reconstruction Off/On) or fanned graphic cards to show quantity (anime cricket cards).
- **Encapsulated Outro Templates**: Housing a widescreen clip inside a vertical template with clean call-to-actions ("FULL VIDEO ON YOUTUBE" / "LINK IN BIO").

### sound_design_sync
- **Dialogue-Driven Visual Cuts**: Executing visual cuts and layouts resets immediately following vocal spikes or speech intensity peaks.
- **Graphic Entrance Syncing**: Synchronizing the appearance of floating cards/overlays to audio peaks (DiscussingFilm tweet entry, Tim Sweeney overlay entry).
- **Segment Pivots on Audio Peaks**: Transitioning between narrative themes and live CES footage on strong vocal emphasis peaks.

### data_text_density
- **Oversized Italic Captions & Centers**: Capturing viewer attention with large, white, italicized uppercase captions for key spoken terms ("SEEING", "NEW MODELS") or huge center-screen text punches ("SaaS is DEAD").
- **Datasheets & Complex Spec Tables**: Presenting detailed product specifications in tabular format (GeForce RTX 5070 vs older gen specification table) or labeled systems diagrams (die layout highlights, Ultraviolette bike architecture systems list).
- **Tilted Screenshots & Thread Overlays**: Displaying dense article cards (CNN Business, GamingBolt) or stacked floating thread cards (Twitter thread with Tim Sweeney's reply) directly on screen for self-paced reading.
