# Channel Profile: channel_1_upgrido_retro_doc

Weighting Matrix Version: v1
Videos Reviewed: 10

## Category Strengths

- **asset_sourcing** (Weight: 9.5): Primary category. Grounded sourcing and preparation workflows documented in multiple videos. Sourcing steps convert script keywords into Pinterest/Cosmos searches, organize candidate assets on Figma moodboards, extract color palettes via Adobe Color, and list exact hex codes. For generative assets, detailed prompt recipes specify era, texture, lighting, and negative constraints to ensure clean subject separation in After Effects layers.
- **asset_selection** (Weight: 9.0): Primary category. High-quality, topic-congruent asset selection that avoids generic B-roll. It favors brand-congruent palettes (such as Apple's black-and-white theme), concrete visual anchors for abstract concepts (like a phone loading spinner for bad internet), movie/genre-specific stills to demonstrate color choices, and classical or historical references (statues, vintage newspaper front pages) to ground style discussions.
- **editing_layout** (Weight: 7.5): Optional category. Excellent spatial sequencing and layout consistency. The channel organizes elements using circular containment grids, groups assets to show ownership, applies 3D motion layouts (such as open-book displays and flipping cards), inserts branded chapter slates as resets, and translates abstract composition principles into geometric diagrams.
- **sound_design_sync** (Weight: 7.0): Optional category. Extremely weak category with minimal evidence. Sync observations are restricted to audio peaks clustering around specific product highlights/ecosystem connections or transition cuts.
- **good_video_principles** (Weight: 6.5): Optional category. Clear instructional hook and pacing structures. Critique videos open by showing a viewer request or a before-state to frame the tutorial as a concrete repair job. Pacing is structured with numbered workflow roadmap cards and talking-head resets, resolving with a final rectified payoff frame that demonstrates all fixes working together.
- **data_text_density** (Weight: 6.0): Low priority category. Typographic and layout density is kept low, using isolated labels, price tags stylized as tickets, simple color psychology tables, and geometric color wheel diagrams to explain color harmony categories visually.

## Repeated Patterns

### asset_selection
- **Brand-Congruent Palettes**: Choosing backgrounds, assets, and typography that reinforce the brand identity of the subject (e.g., Apple's black-and-white theme replacing a low-contrast blue field in video 1 obs 2, obs 3).
- **Concrete Problem Anchors**: Visualizing abstract script points using recognizable real-world objects, such as a black smartphone with a loading spinner representing poor/expensive internet data (video 3 obs 2).
- **Topic-Specific/Genre Stills**: Using specific movie frames (such as dark red-toned film stills or warm village backdrops) to showcase visual and emotional styling categories (video 5 obs 4, obs 5; video 9 obs 6).
- **Classical/Historical Reference Stills**: Anchoring style categories with authentic artifacts, such as classical statues, vintage newspaper front pages, or large letterform crops (video 7 obs 1, obs 2, obs 5; video 10 obs 2).

### asset_sourcing
- **Pinterest/Cosmos Search and Moodboard Compilation**: Converting script keywords directly into visual searches, collecting candidate designs in a Figma workspace, and routing the moodboard into Adobe Color to extract palettes (video 2 obs 3, obs 4, obs 5).
- **Generative Prompt Recipes**: Defining detailed prompts for AI-generated images that specify subject, era, negative constraints (e.g., empty space, no people), and backdrops optimized for subject isolation (video 1 obs 6, video 4 obs 1, obs 3, video 6 obs 7).
- **Linked Layer Separation**: Preparing imported PSD/PNG assets as parented layers linked to nulls in After Effects to separate subjects from backdrops for visual camera movements (video 4 obs 4, obs 7).
- **Named Swatch Sourcing**: Listing hex codes on cards (e.g., Emerald Green #046307) to anchor palette direction into repeatable sourcing assets (video 3 obs 4).

### good_video_principles
- **Before-State Hook Framing**: Starting videos by showcasing the viewer's original edit request before presenting any critique or fix, framing the tutorial as a concrete repair job (video 1 obs 1, video 6 obs 1, video 9 obs 1, video 10 obs 1).
- **Roadmap Workflow Setup**: Using visible numbered workflow markers (e.g., a four-step process sequence) early in the video to guide the audience through a structured tutorial (video 2 obs 1).
- **Payoff/Rectification Frames**: Holding the final, unified story composite as a complete payoff at the end, displaying all corrections working together (video 1 obs 8, video 3 obs 7, video 4 obs 9, video 6 obs 9, video 10 obs 9).
- **Pacing with Resets**: Utilizing talking-head reset cuts periodically between dense diagram-heavy or technical sequences to keep visual pacing digestible (video 8 obs 2).

### editing_layout
- **Grid & Shape Containment**: Arranging cutouts or product images inside circular zones or yellow backing shapes on grid backgrounds to create contrast and alignment (video 1 obs 4, video 3 obs 6).
- **Logical Containment**: Enclosing related assets in a podium or container to visually represent relationships and ownership without relying only on dialogue (video 3 obs 5).
- **3D Spatial Arrangements**: Placing text and image assets as layers in 3D space with depth, camera paths, rotation, and Z-axis keyframing (video 4 obs 6, obs 8, video 6 obs 5, video 6 obs 9).
- **Storybook & Panel Sequencing**: Formatting historical narratives within open-book spreads (assigning geography to the left, brand/date to the right) or using storyboard panels to plan and sequence shots (video 2 obs 7, video 4 obs 5).
- **Cinematic Reset Slates**: Inserting brief, textured branded chapter slates (e.g., red/car-textured 'UNSPOKEN' slate) to separate sections and transition themes (video 5 obs 3, video 9 obs 4, video 10 obs 4).
- **Visualizing Layout Geometry**: Breaking down balanced or depth-based layouts into simplified shapes (triangles, color wheels, circular subject weights in 2, 3, or 5-part zones) to make composition principles explicit (video 8 obs 1, obs 4, obs 5, obs 6, obs 7, video 9 obs 7, obs 8, video 10 obs 3).

### sound_design_sync
- **Audio Peak Accents**: Placing high-amplitude audio peaks on key visual highlights (product details) or visual cuts (workflow-to-presenter transition) to add weight to the transition (video 1 obs 7, video 2 obs 8).

### data_text_density
- **Physical Numeric Props**: Converting simple statistics or prices into textured, physical-feeling assets like yellow ticket stubs with torn edges (video 3 obs 3).
- **Color Psychology Tables**: Using color bands as row backgrounds in tables to compress explanation and reduce reading load (video 5 obs 7).
- **Diagrammatic Harmonization**: Using labeled color-wheel diagrams with geometric markers to explain color harmony categories (complementary, analogous, triadic, monochromatic) visually rather than textually (video 10 obs 6, obs 7, obs 8).
- **Sparse Metadata Labels**: Introducing labels (product names, prices, design headings) sequentially after visual layouts are established, ensuring low density is preserved (video 1 obs 5, video 5 obs 6, video 8 obs 3, video 9 obs 5).

## Notes For Global Synthesis

- **Interactive Sourcing-to-Selection Pipeline**: The channel's signature high-weight categories (asset selection and sourcing) work together as a pipeline: keywords from the script are converted to Pinterest searches, Figma moodboards, and Adobe Color extractions, or used as detailed prompt recipes for AI generation.
- **Composition Visualized as Geometry**: Layout principles (balance, depth, margins) are taught by showing simplified geometric diagrams (triangles, color wheels, circular subject weights on grids) instead of complex verbal instructions.
- **Before-State and payoff payoff structure**: Video pacing relies on establishing the before-state (viewer edit) at the start and holding the unified rectified payoff at the end, maintaining strong tutorial alignment.
- **Pervasive Transcription Failures**: Almost all videos suffer from transcription noise or complete garbling (especially video 8), necessitating a visual-first verification approach for channel-level analysis.
